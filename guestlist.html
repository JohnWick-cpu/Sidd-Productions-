    How it works

    On the list in a minute.

    1. Pick your night

      Choose a dated night or one of the weekly rooftop nights, then tap Get on the list.

    2. Tell us who is coming

      Add your name, your number and your group: how many male, female and couples. Mention a table if you want one.

    3. Send it on WhatsApp

      Your request opens as a ready-to-send WhatsApp message to Sidd Productions.

    4. Get confirmed

      We confirm your entry by message. Keep the confirmation handy at the door.

    Age
    21 and above only, as on every poster. Carry a valid photo ID.
    Venue
    HOD Club, Koramangala, Bengaluru.
    Start time
    Most nights start at 8 PM. Each poster shows that night's time.
    Parking
    Valet parking is available.
    Tables
    Tick the table box in your request and we will share the options on WhatsApp.
    Confirmation
    A request is not an entry until we confirm it by message.
