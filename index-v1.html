    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    What kinds of events does Sidd Productions plan?

    Corporate parties, award nights and galas, team offsites, themed celebrations and club nights across Bengaluru.

    Where do your events take place?

    At Bengaluru's top venues, including SkyDeck by Sherlock's on MG Road, House of Dopamine, Sugar Factory, Mirrors and Blurred. For offsites and private events we can also bring the night to your own location.

    Which parts of Bengaluru do you cover?

    Our venues are in Koramangala and MG Road, two of Bengaluru's hottest neighbourhoods. Tell us where your team wants to celebrate and we will take it from there.

    What does Sidd Productions take care of?

    Everything: the venue, the lights, the music and the vibe. You walk in and watch your team light up.

    Do you offer party packages?

    Yes. The Packages page explains the Private Party, Party and Corporate Gold packages: what is included, how the night runs, how booking works and the full menus.

    How do I check dates and request a booking?

    Use the booking planner on this page. Pick a venue, choose an open night and send your request on WhatsApp. We confirm every date by message.

    Can I check the dates against my own calendar?

    Yes. Drop a calendar file (.ics) into the planner and it shows which open nights you are free. The file is read in your browser and is never uploaded.

    I am a DJ. Can I collaborate with Sidd Productions?

    Yes. Send your styles, the nights you can play and a recent set through the DJs form on this page.

    Who founded Sidd Productions?

    Syed Khashif founded Sidd Productions to create nights people genuinely cannot stop talking about.
