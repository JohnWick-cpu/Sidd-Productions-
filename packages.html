The menu

A table that travels.

A good night deserves a good table. These menus are built the way a considered restaurant builds its own: a bar to open the evening, starters to wake the palate, mains with room to breathe, and something sweet to close.

Across our three packages you will find the tandoor and the coast, the wok and the pizza oven, Thai curries and Mediterranean flavours, with vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes in every course. What you can choose from depends on your package, and the choosing is yours.

Know your menu

A few dishes and terms, explained.

Tikka
Marinated pieces of paneer, chicken or fish, grilled until charred at the edges.
Harissa
A North African chilli paste made with garlic and spices.
Tahina
A rich, nutty paste of ground sesame from the Middle East.
Chimichurri
A bright Argentine sauce of herbs, garlic and vinegar.
Massaman
A mild, rich Thai curry built on warm spices.
Thai green curry
A fragrant coconut curry made with fresh green chillies and herbs.
Chettinad
The peppery, spice-roasted cooking of Tamil Nadu's Chettinad region.
Donne biryani
A Bengaluru-style biryani, traditionally served in a leaf bowl called a donne.
Mangalorean
Cooking from the coast of Karnataka, often led by coconut.
Guntur
A town in Andhra Pradesh famous for its chillies, so expect heat.
Natti style
Kannada for country style, as in the pepper fries.
Manchow
A hot, tangy Indo-Chinese soup, finished with crisp noodles.
Schezwan
A fiery chilli and garlic sauce from the Indo-Chinese kitchen.
Alfredo and Arrabiata
Two classic pasta sauces: Alfredo is creamy and buttery, Arrabiata is a spicy tomato.
Kamal kakdi
Lotus stem, with a crisp bite.
Do pyaza
A curry built around onions, cooked two ways.
Gyoza
Japanese-style pan-fried dumplings.
Tres leches
A sponge cake soaked in three kinds of milk.
Gulkand
A sweet preserve of rose petals.
Gajar halwa
A slow-cooked carrot dessert, rich and warming.

Questions

Good to know before you book.

What does a package include?

Each package covers drinks, starters, a main course and dessert, and we run the night around it. The details differ by package, so use the comparison above to see how they line up.

Can I choose my own dishes?

Yes. Every package lists how many dishes you choose from each section, for example any 3 vegetarian and any 3 non-vegetarian starters in the Private Party package.

What cuisines are on the menu?

Across the three packages: North Indian tandoor dishes and gravies, regional cooking from Karnataka, Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh, Indo-Chinese, Thai curries, Italian pizza and pasta, Mediterranean and Tex-Mex flavours, Asian dumplings and tofu, bar bites and desserts.

Can I read the full menu before I decide?

Yes. Every package has its full menu on this page. Open “See the full menu” under a package to read each dish, and use the menu guide to see how many dishes you choose from in each course.

Are there vegetarian and non-vegetarian options?

Both. Starters and main courses are listed separately for vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes, so you choose the mix that suits your group.

How long does a package run?

The Private Party and Party packages run for three hours: drinks and starters for the first 90 minutes, then the main course for the last 90. For Corporate Gold, timing is confirmed when you book.

Do I need to pay an advance?

For the Private Party and Party packages, a 50% advance blocks the table and confirms your party date. For Corporate Gold, we confirm the booking terms with you.

What is not part of the package?

Anything consumed outside the package inclusions is charged as per actuals at the end of the party. In the Party package, Red Bull, Tonic Water, bottled water and Diet Pepsi are chargeable extras. In the Private Party package, Diet Pepsi is chargeable.

What if more guests arrive than we confirmed?

With the Corporate Gold package, the per-head rate applies to any additional guests beyond the confirmed count.

Can we mix packages at one party?

With the Corporate Gold package, only one package is offered per group, so everyone at the party is on the same package.

Until when does the music play?

Service hours follow Bengaluru law. Music switches off at the stipulated time and the outlet closes as per Bengaluru bar operational hours law.

How do I check dates and book?

Use the booking planner on the home page to see open nights and pick a venue, then send your request on WhatsApp.
